
# Electro-SKU

### Smart SKU Management System | An Electro Rescue Initiative

> Organizing electronic components, managing inventory, and supporting the circular electronics ecosystem.

## About the Project

**Electro-SKU** is a project under **Electro Rescue**, designed to support the systematic identification, classification, and management of electronic components and products.

The project aims to simplify component tracking, improve inventory organization, and make electronic component management more structured and efficient.

Electro-SKU aligns with Electro Rescue's vision of promoting the reuse, recovery, and responsible management of electronic resources.

## Vision

To build a structured and scalable system for managing electronic components, enabling better traceability, organization, and utilization of electronic resources.

## Key Objectives

- Organize electronic components systematically.
- Maintain structured component information.
- Simplify component identification and tracking.
- Improve inventory management.
- Support component reuse and recovery.
- Build a scalable foundation for electronic resource management.

## Features

- **Component Management:** Maintain organized records of electronic components.
- **SKU Identification:** Support unique identification of products and components.
- **Inventory Organization:** Structure component information for easier management.
- **Traceability:** Enable better tracking of electronic resources.
- **Scalability:** Designed with future expansion in mind.

## How It Works

The intended workflow of Electro-SKU is:

1. **Component Entry:** Add electronic components or products to the system.
2. **Identification:** Assign or associate a unique SKU identifier.
3. **Classification:** Organize components based on relevant categories and specifications.
4. **Record Management:** Maintain structured component information.
5. **Tracking:** Retrieve and manage component records when required.

## About Electro Rescue

**Electro Rescue** is an AI-powered e-waste management initiative focused on responsible electronic waste collection, PCB assessment, component recovery, recycling, and reuse.

Its mission is to give discarded electronics a second life by identifying valuable components and encouraging a circular electronics economy.
